Desk Mail

A fast, keyboard-first email and calendar app for the Mac.

Desk Mail is a desktop email client. It works with Gmail and Google Calendar, Outlook.com and Microsoft 365, iCloud, and any IMAP mail server. You sign in with your provider in your own browser, and the app keeps your mail on your own computer.

What it does

  • Reads, sorts, searches, sends and archives your email, with keyboard shortcuts for everything.
  • Shows new mail and sign-in codes within seconds, with notifications.
  • Shows your calendar beside your mail: answer invitations, create events, and share times you're free.
  • Optional AI features (summaries, suggested replies, search in plain words) that run only when you use them.

Your data

Desk Mail has no servers. Your mail, calendar and sign-in stay on your Mac, and the app talks directly to your email provider. Read the privacy policy for the details, including how Google user data is used.
