Privacy policy

Desk Mail · Last updated 17 November 2026

Desk Mail is a desktop email and calendar app that runs on your Mac. This policy explains what data it accesses, how it uses that data, and where the data goes.

No servers

Desk Mail has no servers of its own and no account system. It does not collect analytics or telemetry. The developer never receives your email, calendar, contacts or sign-in.

What the app accesses

When you add an account, you sign in with your provider (Google, Microsoft or another) in your own browser and choose to grant access. With that access, Desk Mail:

  • Gmail (https://mail.google.com/): reads your messages and labels to show them, sends mail you write, and applies the changes you make (archive, label, delete, mark read).
  • Google Calendar (events, calendar list, free/busy): shows your events, answers invitations, creates and edits events you ask for, and finds times you're free.
  • Basic profile (email address, name): identifies which account you signed in with.

Where your data is stored

Messages, events and search indexes are stored only on your Mac, in the app's data folder. Sign-in tokens are stored in your Mac's Keychain. You can remove an account at any time in Settings, which deletes its data from your Mac, and you can revoke the app's access in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Who data is shared with

Desk Mail talks directly to your email and calendar providers. It does not sell, rent or share your data, and does not use it for advertising.

The only exception is the optional AI features (summaries, suggested replies, plain-language search). They run only when you turn them on or use them, and only on the messages involved. The text of those messages is then sent to the AI provider you choose in Settings, or processed by a model on your Mac if you choose the local option. Desk Mail does not use your data to train AI models.

Remote images in emails load only when you allow them, through the app itself, so senders can't track when you open a message unless you choose to load them.

Google user data

Desk Mail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the email and calendar features described above, in the app on your Mac. It is not transferred to anyone except as described in this policy, not used for advertising, and not read by people except with your permission, for security reasons, or where the law requires it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
